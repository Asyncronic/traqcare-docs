The Traqcare mobile app now supports 14 white-label brand flavors, each with custom app icons, splash screens, and branding.

<!-- truncate -->

## Supported Brands

Each brand gets its own:
- Custom app icon and splash screen
- Brand-specific color scheme
- Separate app store listing
- Independent Firebase push notification configuration

## For Resellers

If you're a reseller interested in a white-label version of the Traqcare platform, the setup includes:
- Custom mobile app branding (iOS + Android)
- Branded web app login page
- Custom subdomain
- Branded report headers

Contact support through the **Tickets** section in your dashboard to get started.
