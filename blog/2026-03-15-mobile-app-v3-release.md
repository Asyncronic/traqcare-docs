The Traqcare mobile app has been updated with major new features across tracking, reporting, and localization.

<!-- truncate -->

## Highlights

### 13 Languages Supported

The app is now available in English, Arabic, Portuguese, Vietnamese, French, German, Spanish, Indonesian, Hindi, Nepali, Persian, Pashto, and Serbian. Change your language in **More > Settings > Language**.

### 3D Follow Mode

When tracking a single vehicle, enable **3D Follow Mode** to get a driver's-eye perspective. The camera tilts and rotates to follow the vehicle's heading in real time.

### 9 Report Types on Mobile

Generate and view reports directly on your phone:
1. Daily Travel Summary
2. Daily Distance
3. Stoppage Summary
4. Trip Summary
5. Alert Report
6. Fuel Summary (with interactive chart)
7. Speed & Distance
8. Idle Report
9. Travel Summary

### Quick Date Selection

Playback now shows quick date chips at the bottom of the screen — tap "Today", "Yesterday", or any recent date to instantly load the route.

### Voice Alerts

Enable **Voice Alerts** in settings to receive spoken notifications when alerts trigger — useful for drivers who can't look at their phone.
