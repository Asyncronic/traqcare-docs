Traqcare now supports live video streaming and recorded footage playback from vehicle-mounted cameras.

<!-- truncate -->

## Features

### Live Video Streaming

Watch real-time video from vehicle dashcams directly in the Traqcare web app. Supports:
- **Traqcare native video** — stream directly within the platform
- **Traqcare Embedded** — use Traqcare server credentials on external platforms
- **Third-party platforms** — bring your own video server credentials

### Recorded Footage Playback

Review past footage with a timeline interface:
- Navigate to any date and time
- Scrub through the timeline to find specific moments
- Multi-camera view for vehicles with multiple cameras (1, 4, 9, or 16 channels)

### Device Snapshots

View captured images from device cameras in the Object Gallery.

## Setup

Camera support requires a camera sensor to be configured on the GPS device. See our [Camera Settings Guide](/docs/device-management/camera-settings) for setup instructions.
