A dedicated Geofence Dashboard is now available on the web platform, giving you deeper insights into zone activity.

<!-- truncate -->

## What's New

The Geofence Dashboard provides analytics for all your configured zones:

- **Entry/Exit event counts** per geofence — see which zones are most active
- **Visit frequency charts** — identify patterns in zone usage over time
- **Dwell time analysis** — understand how long vehicles spend inside each zone
- **Map visualization** — see all geofences with activity data overlaid on the map

## Use Cases

- **Delivery operations** — track how often drivers visit customer sites and how long they stay
- **Construction** — monitor equipment dwell time at job sites
- **Fleet compliance** — ensure vehicles don't enter restricted zones

Access the Geofence Dashboard from **Dashboard > Geofence Dashboard** in the web app sidebar.

Learn more in our [Geofences Guide](/docs/geofences).
