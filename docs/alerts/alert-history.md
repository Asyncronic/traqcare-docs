# Alert History

Every alert that fires is saved, so you can check what happened, when and where. Use the notification bell or the mobile Alerts list for the last 24 hours, and the Alert Report for any other date range. Alerts are kept for 90 days.

![Alert Report](/img/screenshots/web/alert-report.png)

## Recent alerts on the web (last 24 hours)

1. Click the **bell icon** in the top bar. A dot on the bell means something new has arrived.
2. The **Alerts** tab shows your six newest alerts from the last 24 hours, each with the vehicle, the alert text, the address and the time.
3. Click an alert to open **Alert Details** with the position on a map. Click **Track Live** to follow the vehicle on the live map.
4. Click **View all** to open the **Notifications** panel. Its **Alerts** table lists every alert from the last 24 hours with **Name**, **Alert**, **Address** and **Time**. Use the search icons on **Name** and **Alert** to filter.

The **Reminders & Messages** tab next to it holds reminders and messages, not vehicle alerts.

## Alert Report on the web (any date range)

1. Open **Reports** in the sidebar and choose **Alert Report**.
2. Select the **Object** (one vehicle per report).
3. Leave **Alert Type** on **All**, or pick one type such as Overspeed, Ignition or Geofence.
4. Pick a range under **Select Time Range** (Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last Week, This Month or Last Month), or set **From** and **To**.
5. Click **Submit**.

The report lists the newest alerts first with **Imei**, **Alert Time** (device time), **Server Time**, **Alert**, **Speed**, **Location** and **Coordinates**. The map icon in the **Map** column shows the point on a map. You can export the table to CSV or Excel. For totals per vehicle and alert type across several vehicles, use the Alert Count Report described in [Additional reports](../reports/additional-reports.md). Report details are on the [Alert Report](../reports/alert-report.md) page.

## Alerts on the mobile app

- **All vehicles, last 24 hours:** **More > Alerts & Reminders > Alerts**. Filter by alert type (**All Alerts**) and vehicle (**All Devices**), search by alert, vehicle name or IMEI, and narrow the date. Tap an alert to see it on a map.
- **One vehicle:** tap the **Alerts** button on the vehicle's map screen. Vehicles with a dashcam also show an **AI** tab.
- **From a push notification:** tapping it opens the Alerts screen for that vehicle.
- **Longer periods:** open **Reports** for the vehicle (from its entry in **List** or from its map screen), then tap **Alerts**. The report opens on the last seven days; change the dates at the top or use Today, Yesterday, This Week or Last Week.

## Common questions

### How long are alerts kept?
90 days. Older alerts are deleted automatically and no longer appear in any list or report.

### Why does the Alert Report show alerts I never set up?
The Alert Report shows everything recorded for the vehicle. That includes alarms the tracker raises on its own, such as SOS, even when nobody has an alert for them, and alerts that other users have set up on the same vehicle. The bell and the mobile Alerts list only show alerts set up for your user.

### Why can't I see alerts older than one day in the bell?
The bell and the mobile Alerts list only load the last 24 hours. Use the Alert Report for anything older.

### Is there a history of dashcam AI alerts?
Yes. AI alerts from dashcams have their own page under **Alerts > AI Alerts** on the web, and an **AI** tab on the vehicle's Alerts screen in the mobile app.
