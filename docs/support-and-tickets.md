# Support & Tickets

![Raise New Ticket](/img/screenshots/web/raise-new-ticket.png)

## Creating a Ticket

1. Navigate to **Support Tickets** (Web: sidebar, Mobile: More > Support)
2. Click/tap **Create New Ticket**
3. Enter a **subject** and **description**
4. Select the **type** (Bug Report, Feature Request, Question, etc.)
5. Optionally attach screenshots or files
6. Submit

Track your ticket status (Open, In Progress, Closed) and reply within the ticket thread.

![View Tickets](/img/screenshots/web/view-tickets.png)

## Contact Us (Mobile)

For quick support, use **More > Contact Us** to send a message directly to the support team.
