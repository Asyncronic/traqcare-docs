# Support & Tickets

Support tickets let you report a problem or ask for help from inside Traqcare and keep the whole conversation in one place. A ticket goes to the account above yours: your company's main user, or, if you are the main user, your service provider.

![Raise New Ticket](/img/screenshots/web/raise-new-ticket.png)

## Raise a ticket on the web

1. Click the help (question mark) icon in the top bar and choose **Create Ticket**. You can also choose **View Ticket** and click **Raise Ticket** on the **Open Tickets** tab.
2. In **Raise New Ticket**, fill in:
   - **Title** (required, up to 80 characters).
   - **Category** (required): **Request**, **Complaint**, **Enquiry**, **Renewal** or **Other**.
   - **Description** (required): describe the issue in detail, for example the vehicle name, IMEI and what you expected to happen.
   - **Attachments** (optional): click **Upload Attachments** to add screenshots or photos (image files).
3. Click **Submit Ticket**. The ticket gets a number that starts with **TK**.

## Raise a ticket in the mobile app

1. Open **More > Help & Support > Contact Us**.
2. Tap **Create New Ticket**.
3. Enter the **Title** (up to 80 characters), **Category** and **Description**. Under **Attachments (Optional)** you can add up to 5 images, which are compressed before upload.
4. Tap **Submit Ticket**.

The same screen shows **Email Us**, **Call Us** and **WhatsApp Support** when your service provider has entered a support email or phone number. If none is set, it shows **Support Unavailable**.

## Track and reply to tickets

On the web, the tickets page has two tabs:

- **Open Tickets**: tickets that are **Open** or **In Progress**.
- **Closed Tickets**: tickets that are **Resolved** or **Closed**.

The table shows Ticket #, Created By, Title, Category, Status, Created On and Updated On, with filters for category and status. Click a ticket number to open **Ticket Details**, which shows the description, attachments and **Remarks**. Type in **Add your remarks or resolution** and click **Submit Remark** to reply.

In the mobile app, tap **My Tickets** on the Contact Us screen. The **Open**, **Closed** and **All** tabs list your tickets. Tap one to read the conversation and type a reply.

You get a notification when someone adds a remark or changes the status of your ticket. A closed ticket cannot take new remarks.

![View Tickets](/img/screenshots/web/view-tickets.png)

## Ticket statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | New ticket, not yet picked up |
| **In Progress** | Someone is working on it |
| **Resolved** | The issue has been fixed |
| **Closed** | Finished. Closing needs a reason, which is added to the remarks |

Reseller accounts change the status of tickets raised by their clients with the **Change** menu: **Reopen**, **In Progress**, **Resolved** or **Closed**.

## Common questions

### Who can see my ticket?

You, the admin users of your company, and the accounts above yours. A user account sees only its own tickets. Admin and reseller accounts can switch between **All Tickets**, **My Tickets** and **Client Tickets**.

### Can I close my own ticket?

No. The status is changed by the account that handles your ticket. Add a remark if the problem is solved or if you want it closed.

### Why is there no Call Us or Email Us option in the app?

Those options appear only when your service provider has entered a support email or phone number. Raise a ticket instead, or contact the company you bought the service from.
