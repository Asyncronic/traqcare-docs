# Routes & Trips (Web)

## Routes

Create predefined routes with waypoints and assign them to vehicles:

1. Navigate to **GeoJsons > Routes**
2. Click **Add Route** and draw the route on the map
3. Add waypoints along the path
4. Name the route and save
5. Assign vehicles to the route for deviation monitoring

## Trips

Plan and manage scheduled journeys:
- Create trip schedules with start/end locations
- Assign to specific vehicles and drivers
- Track trip progress against planned routes
