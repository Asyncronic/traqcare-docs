# Mobile-Specific Features

Most of the Traqcare app mirrors the web platform, but some features use the phone itself: its camera, location, speaker, maps apps and share sheet. This page lists those features and where to find each one.

![Send Command](/img/screenshots/mobile/send-command.jpg) ![Share Location](/img/screenshots/mobile/share-location.jpg) ![Device Details](/img/screenshots/mobile/device-details.jpg)

## Features and where to find them

| Feature | Where | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| **QR code scanner** | **More > Reseller > Add Object**, scan button in the IMEI field | Reads the IMEI from the device's QR code or barcode with the camera, or from a photo. Reseller accounts only. |
| **Voice Alerts** | **More > Preferences > Voice Alerts** | Reads alerts aloud in English while the app is open. |
| **Navigate to a vehicle** | Vehicle live map, navigation button | Opens the vehicle's position in Google Maps or Apple Maps (iPhone) or your maps app (Android). |
| **Street View** | Vehicle live map; **Playback > Street View here**; a playback stop card | Opens Google Street View at the vehicle, the playback point or the stop. |
| **Share location** | **List > tap a vehicle > Share** | Sends the current position as a Google Maps link, or a live link for one hour, three hours, one day or one week, through your phone's share sheet. |
| **Add Geofence here** | Vehicle live map, **Add Geofence** | Draws a circle geofence at the vehicle's position or at your phone's position. |
| **Odometer correction** | Vehicle live map, tap **Odometer** | Enter the real odometer reading and tap **Update**. |
| **Live tail** | Vehicle live map, tail button | Shows or hides the vehicle's recent path. The choice is remembered. |
| **Quick date chips** | **Playback** | One tap loads Today, Yesterday or one of the five days before. |
| **Pull to refresh** | **List** tab, alerts list | Swipe down to reload at once. |
| **Landscape charts** | **Reports > Fuel Summary** and **Speed vs Distance** | Charts open in landscape for a wider view. |
| **Send commands** | **List > tap a vehicle > Commands** | Pick a command or a **Custom Command**, tap **Send Command**, and see past commands in **Command History**. |

## Dashcam features

These appear only for devices with a video camera configured. Full steps are in [Video, Audio & Camera](./video-and-camera.md).

- **Monitor**: listen to live audio from the vehicle.
- **Intercom**: talk to the driver through the dashcam.
- **Video history**: from **Playback > Video history**, play recordings stored on the device's SD card, **Upload to Cloud**, or **Download Recording** as MP4. You can leave the screen while an upload or download continues. One search covers at most 24 hours, and fetching the file list uses the device's SIM data.
- **AI alert evidence**: play the video of an ADAS/DSM event inside the app, with a portrait/landscape switch.
- **Gallery**: for devices with a camera sensor, tap a photo to view it full screen and zoom.

When the app goes to the background or the phone locks, the live video streams stop. Open the video page again to restart them.

## Common questions

### Is there a 3D follow mode?

No. The vehicle's live map stays flat and north-up and follows the vehicle.

### Why can't I find the QR scanner?

It is part of the **Add Object** form, which is under **More > Reseller** and only shows for reseller accounts.

### Do voice alerts speak my app language?

No. Voice alerts are always spoken in English, whatever language the app is set to.
