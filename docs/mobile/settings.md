# Mobile Settings

All settings in the Traqcare app are in the **More** tab, which opens a screen titled **Settings**. It has your profile card at the top, then four sections you can collapse by tapping their headings: **Quick Actions**, **Preferences**, **Policies** and **Help & Support**, with **Logout** and the app version at the bottom.

![Mobile More Menu](/img/screenshots/mobile/more.jpg)

## Profile

Tap the profile card (your name, company and role) to open **Profile**:

- **Account Details**: contact person, email, phone, country and your provider's support contacts. Tap the edit icon to change them.
- **Change Password**: set a new password and tap **Save**.

## Quick Actions

| Action | What it opens |
|--------|---------------|
| **Reseller** | Reseller accounts only: **Add Client**, **Add Object**, **Migrate Objects**, **Activate/Renew** and **Transfer Coins**. |
| **Geofence** | Your geofences. See [Geofences & POI](./geofences-and-poi.md). |
| **Point of Interest** | Your POIs. |
| **Alerts & Reminders** | **Alerts** (last 24 hours) and **Reminder & Messages**. See [Mobile Alerts](./alerts.md). |

## Preferences

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Notification** | Turns push notifications on or off for this phone. |
| **Voice Alerts** | Reads alerts aloud (in English) when they arrive while the app is open. On by default. |
| **Include Sub Clients** | Reseller accounts only. Adds your sub-clients' vehicles to the Dashboard, List and Map. |
| **Alerts Configuration** | Create, edit and delete your alerts. |
| **Home Screen** | The tab the app opens on: **Dashboard**, **List View** or **Map View**. |
| **Preferred Language** | The app language. 26 languages are listed, each in its own script. |
| **Distance Unit** | **Kilometers** or **Miles**, used for distance and speed throughout the app. |
| **Time Zone** | The time zone used for your account. Search the list and tap one. |

### Change a preference

1. Open **More** and find **Preferences** (tap the heading if the section is collapsed).
2. For a switch (**Notification**, **Voice Alerts**, **Include Sub Clients**), tap it to turn it on or off.
3. For a choice (**Home Screen**, **Preferred Language**, **Distance Unit**, **Time Zone**), tap the row and pick a value. The new value shows on the row.

**Notification**, **Voice Alerts**, **Home Screen** and **Preferred Language** are stored on this phone only. **Time Zone** is saved to your user account; if that fails you see "Couldn't update time zone. Please try again." **Distance Unit** is saved on the phone and to your company's account settings; if the server update fails the app keeps the unit on this phone and tells you it couldn't sync.

## Policies and help

- **Policies > Terms of Use** and **Privacy Policy** show the documents in the app.
- **Help & Support > Contact Us** lets you create a support ticket (up to 5 images per ticket), view **My Tickets**, and **Share Logs** with support. See [Support & Tickets](../support-and-tickets.md).

## Log out

Tap **Logout** at the bottom of the More tab. The app shows "Logging out..." and returns to the login screen.

## Common questions

### Why don't I see Reseller or Include Sub Clients?

Both only appear when your company account is a reseller account.

### I changed the language. Why are some words still in English?

Text that has not been translated yet for your language falls back to English. Voice alerts are always spoken in English.

### Does changing the language on my phone change it on the web?

No. The app language is kept on the phone. Set the web language separately on the web platform.
