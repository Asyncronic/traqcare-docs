# Mobile App Overview

The Traqcare mobile app lets you watch your fleet from an Android phone or iPhone: live vehicle status on a map and in a list, route playback, alerts as push notifications, per-vehicle reports, and dashcam video where a camera is installed. You sign in with the same Traqcare account you use on the web.

## Download

| Platform | Link |
|----------|------|
| **Android** | [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.traqcare.gps_tracking_app) |
| **iOS** | [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/traqcare/id6449219819?l=en) |

## Sign in

1. Install the app from the store link above and open it.
2. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
3. Leave **Server** set to `default` unless your provider has given you a different server name.
4. Tap **Login**. The app opens on the Dashboard, or on the tab you choose later as your **Home Screen**.

You can choose the app language on the login screen before you sign in, and change it later in **More > Preferences > Preferred Language**.

## Navigation: the four tabs

The app has four tabs at the bottom of the screen. On a tablet (screen 600 points wide or more) the same four tabs appear as a rail on the left.

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Dashboard** | Vehicle status chart, recent alerts and a small live map. See [Mobile Dashboard](./dashboard.md). |
| **List** | Every vehicle with its status, speed, address and last update. Tap a vehicle for its actions. See [Mobile Tracking](./tracking.md). |
| **Map** | All vehicles on Google Maps, grouped into clusters when zoomed out. |
| **More** | Your profile, Geofence, Point of Interest, Alerts & Reminders, preferences, policies, Contact Us and Logout. See [Mobile Settings](./settings.md). |

Playback, reports, commands and video are per vehicle, so you open them from a vehicle rather than from the More tab: **List > tap a vehicle**, then pick **Playback**, **Reports**, **Commands** and so on.

## What the app can do

- **Live tracking** with status colours: Moving (green), Idle (yellow), Stopped (red), Offline (blue). See [Mobile Tracking](./tracking.md).
- **Route playback** for any period, with trips, stops and speed figures. See [Mobile Playback](./playback.md).
- **Alerts** as push notifications, with an optional spoken read-out. See [Mobile Alerts](./alerts.md).
- **Eight per-vehicle reports**, each downloadable as a PDF. See [Mobile Reports](./reports.md).
- **Geofences and points of interest** drawn on the map. See [Geofences & POI](./geofences-and-poi.md).
- **Dashcam features** (live video, Monitor, Intercom, video history, AI alerts) on vehicles with a video camera configured. See [Video, Audio & Camera](./video-and-camera.md).

## Reseller brands

Resellers can publish the app under their own name, logo and colours. A branded app works the same way as the Traqcare app described in these pages.

## Common questions

### Is the mobile app a separate account from the web platform?

No. You sign in with the same username and password as the web platform, and you see the same vehicles.

### Why can't I find Reports or Playback in the More tab?

They belong to a single vehicle. Open the **List** tab, tap the vehicle, and choose **Playback** or **Reports** from its action sheet.

### Does the app work on tablets?

Yes. On wider screens the bottom tabs move to a navigation rail on the left side of the screen.
