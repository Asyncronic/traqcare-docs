# Mobile App Overview

The Traqcare mobile app brings the full power of fleet tracking to your phone.

**Platforms:** iOS & Android

## Navigation — 4 Bottom Tabs

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Dashboard** | Fleet status overview with charts |
| **List View** | All vehicles with live status |
| **Map View** | All vehicles on the map |
| **More** | Settings, Geofences, POI, Alerts, Reports, Support |

## Supported Languages

English, Arabic, Portuguese, Vietnamese, French, German, Spanish, Indonesian, Hindi, Nepali, Persian, Pashto, and Serbian.

## White-Label Brands

The app is available under multiple brand names: Traqcare, GPSN, BlackOps GPS, BliGPS, Gaitor, Epicera, Revotrack, iTtrack, Geovigile, TezzGPS, Mtrack, Active247GPS, Navicore, and HydaLeye.
